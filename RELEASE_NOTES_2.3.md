# myPC myRight - Release Notes 2.3 (Chrome Web Store)

Copy the block for each edition into the Web Store listing (Store listing > Description, or the
"What's new" section at the top of the description).

---

## myPC myRight Pro - 2.2 -> 2.3

```
What's new in 2.3
• Fixed: Website Exclusions are now fully respected. Excluded sites (including the default docs.google.com) are completely untouched by the extension.
• Exclusions now accept full URLs and paths – e.g. https://docs.google.com/ or www.google.com/maps to exclude only that section of a site.
• Fixed: Selecting cells in Google Sheets and clicking/dragging in Google Docs.
• Fixed: Dragging and panning the map on Google Maps.
• Fixed: Drag interactions on drawing canvases, sliders and video players.
```

---

## myPC myRight (Public) - 2.1 -> 2.3

```
What's new in 2.3
• Exclusions now accept full URLs and paths – e.g. https://docs.google.com/ or www.google.com/maps to exclude only that section of a site.
• Fixed: Selecting cells in Google Sheets and clicking/dragging in Google Docs.
• Fixed: Dragging and panning the map on Google Maps.
• Fixed: Drag interactions on drawing canvases, sliders and video players.
• Fixed: Right-click staying blocked on some sites.
• Fixed: Forced text selection and copy not working on some sites.
• Fixed: Settings not saving on computers where Chrome Sync is turned off. Your existing settings are kept automatically.
```
